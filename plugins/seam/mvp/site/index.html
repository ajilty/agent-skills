The work is in the seams.

Your real projects run across mail, chat, calendar and trackers. No single app can see the through-line — so you hold it in your head, and things fall through. Seam reads the surfaces you already use and puts what actually needs you on one page.

Runs on your machine · plain files you own · reads only, sends nothing

You are not disorganised. Your work is distributed.

A vendor negotiation lives in an email chain, a legal DM, a calendar invite and a tracker page. Four surfaces, one piece of work, and the only place it exists whole is your memory. That is where the cost is — not in any one inbox, but in the reassembly you do every morning.

Asks that went quiet

You delegated it, they said "midweek", it is Thursday and nobody has told you. Seam watches your sent mail for the ask and the silence after it.

Dates said in passing

The deadline that mattered was one clause in the fourth paragraph of a Tuesday email. Seam extracts it and shows you the sentence it came from.

Manufactured urgency

"Pricing expires Friday" from a seller is not the same as a deadline from your own finance team. Seam marks the difference instead of laundering it.

Built by someone who would have to review it.

An aggregator that reads untrusted mail and holds the power to act is a social engineering amplifier. Seam is designed around that, and the guarantees are code plus tests — not a paragraph in a policy.

enforced

Reads only. This release holds no write capability at all. A build lint fails if a send-capable tool name appears anywhere in the source.

enforced

Synced text is data, never instructions. A message telling the system to create a rule produces nothing — and the attempt is logged where you can see it.

enforced

External urgency is marked and capped. A sender's claimed deadline can never reach the top tier alone.

enforced

Facts from outside never auto-promote. Including useful ones. Especially useful ones.

enforced

Every event names its actor. "What did this do while I was away" is answerable line by line, in a file you can grep.

enforced

Work and personal never mix. Seam refuses to start if pointed at a store belonging to another profile.

No account. No server. No telemetry. Your messages never leave the machine and the folder you chose.

Who it is for

People who owe answers

Leaders whose day is other people's asks — where the failure mode is not forgetting a task but forgetting a person.

People with four inboxes

If your work spans mail, chat, a tracker and a calendar, the reassembly cost is real and daily.

People who would audit this

If "an AI reads my mailbox" makes you want to see the source, the ledger, and the test suite — all three are in the box.

What it can't do yet

  • It cannot send, reply, post, or put anything on your calendar. Not gated — absent.
  • Chat is not connected in this release. Mail and calendar only.
  • No drift detection against a tracker — that needs tracker access it does not have.
  • Weekly rollups and reporting are not built.
  • It is one person's overlay. It is not a team product, and it has no multi-user story.
  • Day one it knows nothing about you. It says so on the page rather than faking confidence.
